# One sentence

Hey {{FIRST_NAME}},

This week's exercise is short. One sentence.

**What is the thing OD9 must NEVER fail at, even if it has to abandon everything else?**

Constraints:
- One sentence. (Not a paragraph. Not three bullet points. One real sentence.)
- No platitudes. ("Help humanity flourish" doesn't count. Too vague to fail at.)
- The community will hold you to it. If you write it, expect to be reminded of it later.

This is the hardest question in any campaign, ours included. Most movements never write it down. They drift, optimize for survival, and end up at whatever shape the surrounding pressures push them into. The few that DO write it down — and re-read it routinely — are the ones that survive past their founders.

Some attempts I've seen:

- *"Treat coordination as a design problem, not a moral one."*
- *"Never let aesthetic recruitment outpace genuine engagement."*
- *"Reject the framing that humanity must choose between technology and wisdom."*
- *"Make resource transparency non-negotiable, even when it costs."*
- *"Hold the manifesto's claims to a higher standard of precision than the surrounding noise."*
- *"Be the place that admits its own mistakes faster than its critics can name them."*

None of those are right. None of them are wrong. The work is finding the one *you* would die on.

## What the Manifesto pushes on

**Ch.5** (*The System in Crisis*) and **Ch.55** of the Manifesto take a position you don't see in many big-tent movements: **a project's identity is what it WON'T trade away under pressure.** Everything else is negotiable — strategy, tactics, branding, even most policies. But the one or two non-negotiables determine what the project is.

Most movements get this wrong by trying to make EVERYTHING non-negotiable, which means in practice nothing is — when the pressure comes, something has to give, and without a hierarchy it's whatever's most expedient.

So a Type-I civilization-aware project has to do this exercise explicitly. Not because the answer is permanent (it isn't), but because not doing it means the answer gets chosen by drift. And drift is reliably worse than a deliberately-chosen answer would have been.
