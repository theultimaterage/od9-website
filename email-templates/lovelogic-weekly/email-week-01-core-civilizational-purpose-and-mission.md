# What's left after OD9?

Hey {{FIRST_NAME}},

Thought experiment we're going to keep coming back to over the next 33 weeks:

Imagine OD9 disappears in 100 years. Not the brand — the whole project. Discord, website, Patreon, the manifesto, the people. Gone. Only one thing survives: **a single artifact**. A tool, a habit, a phrase, an institution, anything.

What do you want that artifact to be?

If you can answer that, you've answered the only question that actually matters: **what are we for?**

## What most movements get wrong

Most movements organize against something. Easier to mobilize against a thing than around an absence. They spend years fighting, win some battles, then collapse — because being against something doesn't tell you what to build.

OD9 is supposed to be the opposite. [**Ch.1**](https://offda9.com/curriculum/observer/the-creed.php) of the Manifesto puts it like this: humanity needs to consciously evolve toward Type 1 Kardashev civilization, navigating the Great Filter through systematic transformation of technology, social systems, and consciousness.

Translated: we're not running from the dumpster fire. We're trying to build what comes after.

That's a much harder pitch. "We're against extraction" packs into a tweet. "We're optimizing for civilizational coordination across multi-generational timescales" doesn't. Which is exactly why we keep asking ourselves what success looks like — concretely, in artifacts, in institutions, in actual habits — or we'll drift into being just another anti-thing.
