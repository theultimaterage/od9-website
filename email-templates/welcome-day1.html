What are we for?

{{username}}, a thought experiment we'll keep coming back to: imagine OD9 disappears in 100 years. Not the brand — the whole project. Discord, the site, the music, the people. Gone. Only one artifact survives — a tool, a habit, a phrase, an institution. Anything.

What do you want that artifact to be? Answer that, and you've answered the only question that actually matters: what are we for?

Most movements organize against something — it's easier to mobilize against a thing than around an absence. They win some battles, then collapse, because being against something never tells you what to build. OD9 is the opposite. We're not running from the dumpster fire. We're building what comes after.
